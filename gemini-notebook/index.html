What It Does

A notebook that has actually read everything inside it.

Six things Gemini Notebook does the moment your sources land — no prompt engineering, no copy-pasting between tabs.

Bring Everything In

PDFs, Docs, slides, web links, YouTube transcripts, pasted text and voice notes — up to 50 sources in a single notebook.

Grounded Answers

Every reply is built only from your sources and carries an inline citation — click it and jump straight to the paragraph it came from.

Instant Briefing Docs

One tap turns forty pages into a briefing note, a timeline, an FAQ or a study guide you can hand to somebody else.

Audio Overviews

Turn a notebook into a two-host conversation you can listen to on the drive home — in English or Bahasa Melayu.

Mind Maps & Links

See how ideas across separate documents connect — contradictions, repeated themes and gaps become visible at a glance.

Share The Notebook

Invite your team or class. Everyone asks their own questions against the same trusted set of sources.

How It Works

Three steps, about four minutes.

  1. 01

    Add your sources

    Drag in the files you already have. Nothing needs renaming, tagging or tidying up first.

  2. 02

    Ask in your own words

    Type in English or Bahasa Melayu. "Apa risiko utama dalam laporan ini?" works exactly as well as the English version.

  3. 03

    Turn it into something usable

    Save the answer as a note, generate a briefing doc, or render an audio overview — then share the whole notebook.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Does it work fully in Bahasa Melayu?

Yes. The whole interface switches with the BI / BM toggle in the menu, and you can ask questions and receive answers in Bahasa Melayu even when your sources are in English.

Can it invent facts that are not in my files?

Answers are generated from your uploaded sources and every claim carries a citation you can open. If something is not in your sources, the notebook says so instead of guessing.

What file types can I upload?

PDF, Word, Google Docs and Slides, plain text and Markdown, web pages, YouTube links, and audio recordings such as interviews or meeting notes.

Who can see my notebook?

Only you, until you share it. Sharing is per notebook, and you choose whether collaborators can just read or also add sources.

Is there a free tier?

Yes — you can create notebooks and ask questions at no cost, with higher source limits and longer audio overviews on the paid plan.
