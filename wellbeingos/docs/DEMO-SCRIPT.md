# Executive demonstration — 5 to 7 minutes

The arc: **medical benefits → visibility → wellbeing → intelligence → leadership.**

Open the sign-in screen. The persona picker at the bottom switches roles
instantly; there is no need to sign out between scenes.

---

### Scene 1 — The employee (60s)
Sign in as **Aina Rahman**.

> "This is what a staff member sees on their phone."

Point at the ring: **RM2,500 entitlement, ~78% used, balance remaining**. Note
the amber "Reminder" banner above it — she has crossed the 75% band and has been
told supportively, not warned.

### Scene 2 — Their own record (45s)
**My Benefit** → the transaction ledger, with a running balance after each
approved entry. Then **Panel Clinics** — where she can be seen, and directions.

> "Nothing to request from HR. It is simply there."

### Scene 3 — The clinic (75s)
Switch to **Klinik Sejahtera Kuching — front desk** → **Clinic Portal**.

Type `STIDC1000` → **Verify**. Eligibility confirmed, balance shown.

> "That is the entire set of information the clinic receives. Not her other
> visits, not her wellbeing, not her grade."

Record a **Specialist referral** for **420**. Note the live policy decision line,
then **Submit**. A reference is issued.

### Scene 4 — The decision (45s)
Switch to **Noraini Hassan (HR)** → **Claims & Transactions**. Search the
reference, open it, **Verify**, then **Approve**. The approval timeline records
who did what and when.

### Scene 5 — The wallet updates (30s)
Back to **Aina Rahman** → **My Benefit**.

The balance has moved, and the banner has escalated from *Reminder* to
*Important* — she crossed 90% because of a visit recorded minutes ago.

> "No year-end surprise. She knows today."

### Scene 6 — HR and Finance (60s)
**Noraini Hassan** → **Exceptions**. A live queue: thresholds crossed, duplicate
submissions, values outside the usual range, ageing approvals — each with an
owner and a history.

Switch to **Lim Chee Keong (Finance)** → committed versus approved, projected
year-end, and recovery exposure.

> "This is the year being managed, instead of reconciled in December."

### Scene 7 — Management (60s)
Switch to **STIDC Management**.

Trajectory, monthly utilisation, distribution across the workforce, division
breakdown. Then scroll to the wellbeing signal card.

> "Note what is *not* here. No names. No diagnoses. Divisions below five people
> show 'Suppressed' rather than a number, because a rounded number can still
> identify someone."

### Scene 8 — A pattern appears (45s)
**Wellbeing Intelligence**. Open a signal — musculoskeletal utilisation rising.
Four steps: signal → possible organisational insight → recommended response →
how it would be measured.

> "It does not diagnose anyone. It says: something in how we work may need
> attention."

### Scene 9 — The response (45s)
Switch to **Sarina Abdullah (Wellbeing)**. From the signal, **Launch a programme
in response**. It appears in the intervention register with a named decision-maker.

### Scene 10 — Leadership (45s)
Back to **Management** → participation and outcome movement against the baseline.
Then **ESG Value** — with the evidence register showing what each claim is based
on, and nothing modelled.

---

### The close — the architecture point (60s)
Sign in as **System Administrator** → **Configuration centre**.

Change the entitlement to **RM4,000**; show the employee wallet re-derive. Change
a colour; watch the platform re-skin. Then **Tenants → Switch to Sarawak Agency B**.

> "Different organisation, different name, different branding, RM3,000
> entitlement, thresholds at 60/80/95/100, no wellbeing pulse, stricter privacy
> floor. No code was written. STIDC is the first implementation, not the limit."

*(Administration → Tenants → Regenerate demonstration data resets everything
before the next demonstration.)*
