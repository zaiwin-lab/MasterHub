Our Story

Rooted in Sarawak, growing for the future

Established in 2019, SJF Enterprise (Reg. No. 116118) is a dedicated Bumiputera enterprise operating in the modern agricultural sector, based in Santubong, Kuching, Sarawak.

We were founded to solve a simple, critical need: meeting market demand for consistently fresh, high-quality local produce.

By combining systematic farming methods with a commitment to our local economy, we have built a progressive agricultural business that creates real jobs and delivers real value to the community.

Quality First. An uncompromising focus on producing premium, high-grade agricultural yields.

Systematic Operations. Every farm activity is managed through highly organised, professional and modern frameworks.

Community Impact. Creating employment opportunities and contributing directly to the economic development of our local community.

Premium Harvest

From greenhouse to market, every day

Inside our facilities, agriculture is treated as an exact science — modern fertigation and systematic farm management optimise plant health, minimise waste, and guarantee a uniform, high-quality harvest.

Cili Geronong (Habanero)

Our flagship crop, cultivated under strict quality controls. By managing the growing environment precisely, we deliver chillies with exceptional colour, size, and heat — meeting the rigorous standards of local suppliers and commercial buyers.

Fresh Leafy Vegetables

Alongside our robust chilli production, SJF Enterprise cultivates premium leafy greens. Our systematic approach ensures every head is crisp, vibrant, and market-ready the moment it leaves the greenhouse.
