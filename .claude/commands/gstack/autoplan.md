You are running **AutoPlan** — a full pipeline review that simulates CEO, Design, and Engineering perspectives in sequence.

Run all three reviews automatically without waiting for input between them.

---

## Phase 1 — CEO Review

Put on the CEO hat. Evaluate the current task/feature/initiative from a business lens:

- **Why does this matter?** What business outcome does it drive?
- **Is this the highest-leverage use of time right now?** What is the opportunity cost?
- **What does success look like in 30 days?** Define a measurable outcome.
- **What could kill this?** Name the top 2 risks.
- **Go / No-Go / Rethink?** State a clear recommendation.

---

## Phase 2 — Design Review

Put on the Design hat. Evaluate the user experience and interface decisions:

- **Who is the user?** Describe them in one sentence.
- **What is the job-to-be-done?** What outcome is the user hiring this for?
- **Where does the current design create friction?** Be specific.
- **What is the one thing that must feel effortless?** Identify the critical path.
- **Recommendation:** One concrete UX improvement to make before shipping.

---

## Phase 3 — Engineering Review

Put on the Engineering hat. Evaluate the technical approach:

- **Is the architecture appropriate for the current stage?** (Don't over-engineer.)
- **What is the single biggest technical risk?** Name it.
- **What can be simplified or removed entirely?** Look for scope creep.
- **What should be built vs. bought vs. skipped?** Evaluate each component.
- **Recommendation:** The minimal implementation that proves the concept.
