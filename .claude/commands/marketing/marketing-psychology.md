## Psychological Principles Toolkit

### Colour Psychology
| Colour | Triggers | Use For |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Red | Urgency, energy, appetite | CTAs, sale banners, food |
| Blue | Trust, calm, competence | Finance, SaaS, healthcare |
| Green | Growth, nature, go | Eco, finance, success states |
| Orange | Enthusiasm, affordability | CTAs, youth brands |
| Black | Luxury, authority | Premium, fashion |
| White | Clean, simple, space | Tech, minimal brands |

**Rule:** Use your brand colour on your primary CTA to train users to look there.

### Cognitive Biases to Leverage

**1. Anchoring**
Show the original price before the sale price. The first number seen anchors perception.
> ~~RM 500~~ → RM 249

**2. Social Proof**
People do what others do. Make popularity visible.
> "Join 2,847 users" / "Most popular plan" badge / Logos of known clients

**3. Loss Aversion**
Losing hurts 2x more than gaining feels good. Frame offers as avoiding loss.
> "Don't miss out" / "Stop losing customers to competitors"

**4. Scarcity & Urgency**
Availability limits drive action. Use only if real.
> "Only 3 spots left" / "Offer ends Friday"

**5. Authority**
Credentials, media mentions, and expertise signals reduce hesitation.
> Certifications, "As seen in...", founder credentials

**6. Reciprocity**
Give something first. People feel compelled to return value.
> Free resource → email capture → warm prospect

**7. The Paradox of Choice**
Too many options → no decision. Limit to 3 max. Highlight one.
> 3-tier pricing with "Recommended" badge on middle tier

**8. Pattern Interrupts**
Scroll-stopping creative breaks the autopilot mode. Unexpected image, bold claim, or unusual layout.

## Layout Psychology

- **F-pattern reading:** Eyes scan across the top, then down the left. Put your most important content there.
- **Above the fold:** Value prop + CTA must be visible without scrolling.
- **Directional cues:** Photos of faces looking toward the CTA increase clicks. Arrows and lines guide the eye.
- **Whitespace:** Increases comprehension by 20%. Never fear blank space.
- **Contrast:** Your CTA must be the most visually distinct element on the page.
