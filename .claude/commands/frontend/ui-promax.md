## THE B2B DECISION-MAKER PSYCHOLOGY

Corporate buyers don't behave like consumers. Understand this before writing a single line.

### What a HR Director thinks when they land on your page:
1. **"Is this legitimate?"** — They check logos, certifications, registration numbers first
2. **"Have others like me used this?"** — Industry-specific social proof > general testimonials
3. **"What's the ROI?"** — They need numbers to justify to their CFO
4. **"What's my risk?"** — Training bond, HRD Corp funding = risk mitigation
5. **"What happens next?"** — Explicit next step. No mystery.
6. **"Can I see results before committing?"** — Calculators, case studies, free assessments

### Emotional triggers for B2B (in order of power):
1. **Fear of falling behind peers** — "Other companies in your industry are already doing this"
2. **Status/career protection** — "Implement this before your CEO asks why you haven't"
3. **Data validation** — Specific numbers (not "many companies") remove doubt
4. **Authority transfer** — "HRD Corp-funded + JPK-certified" = government backs this
5. **Cognitive ease** — "We handle everything" reduces mental load
6. **Social proof from equals** — CEO of similar company size > generic 5-star review

## SECTION-BY-SECTION BUILD GUIDE

### HERO — The 5-Second Test

You have 5 seconds to answer 3 questions:
1. What is this?
2. Why should I care?
3. What do I do next?

**Formula:**
```
[Eyebrow Badge]    → Authority/Category signal
[H1 Headline]      → Bold outcome claim. Use <em> for gold highlight.
                     "Your workers get certified. You pay less than RM200 each."
[Sub-headline]     → How it works + who it's for (1 sentence, specific numbers)
[CTA Primary]      → Action verb + outcome ("Calculate My Free Allocation")
[CTA Secondary]    → Ghost/outlined ("See How It Works")
[Trust micro-copy] → "HRD Corp Registered · JPK Accredited · Kuching, Sarawak"
[Hero Visual]      → Abstract data viz OR dashboard screenshot OR gradient stats
[Stats Row]        → 3 big numbers from verified data
```

**What NOT to do in the hero:**
- ❌ Vague headline: "Empowering Your Workforce" (says nothing)
- ❌ No CTA above fold
- ❌ Generic stock photo of business people shaking hands
- ❌ 3 paragraphs of text before the CTA

### PROBLEM SECTION — Make the Pain Real

Don't just describe the problem. **Quantify it in the reader's currency.**

```
Structure:
  Section header: "The Hidden Costs Your Business Is Absorbing"
  
  Card 1 (RED/DANGER): Attrition cost
    → Headline: "RM26,250 — the cost every time one worker quits"
    → Sub: "That's 7.5 months salary. 100 employees, 16% attrition = RM420K/year out the door."
    
  Card 2 (AMBER): Wasted levy
    → Headline: "Your HRD Corp levy is expiring unused every month"
    → Sub: "Since March 2025, unused levy is deducted at 15% year-end. Most companies claim under 40% of what they're entitled to."
    
  Bridge: "CTAP solves both. Simultaneously."
```

### INTERACTIVE CALCULATOR — The Hook that Qualifies Leads

Calculators are the highest-converting B2B lead tools because:
1. They require investment (user inputs their data = high intent signal)
2. They personalise the pitch (the number is about THEM, not generic)
3. They create "aha" moments that copy alone can't
4. Users share results ("look what I calculated") = organic spread

**Calculator design rules:**
- 3–5 inputs maximum (more = abandonment)
- Results update in real-time as they type
- Use sliders where possible — lower friction than typing
- Results should feel slightly surprising (bigger savings than they expected)
- End with a WhatsApp CTA: "Get your full ROI report free"

  Step 3: Training Begins (Month 1)      → On their site, no disruption

### PROOF SECTION — Specifics Beat Generics

**Tier 1 proof (most credible):**
- Named testimonial: "Ahmad Razif, HR Manager, [Company], Miri" + specific metric
- Case study box: "Before: 22% attrition. After 12 months of CTAP: 14%. Savings: RM312,000."

**Tier 2 proof (second best):**
- Third-party stats with sources: "HRD Corp approved RM2.62B in training funding in 2025 — BERNAMA"
- Government body logos: HRD Corp, JPK/DSD, CIDB Malaysia

**Tier 3 proof (still useful):**
- Enrollment number: "47 companies enrolled since launch"
- Certificate count: "1,240 SKM certificates issued"
- Industries served: icons for 8 sectors

**Proof placement rule:** Place your strongest proof JUST BEFORE your primary CTA — it's the final psychological push before the ask.

---

### OBJECTION-HANDLING FAQ

Write FAQs as if you're reading the reader's mind. Sequence by anxiety level, highest first:

1. "Is this really free? What's the catch?" → address cost immediately
2. "What if they complete training and leave?" → training bond answer
3. "How is this different from public TVET?" → key differentiator
4. "My company only has 25 employees. Do I qualify?" → remove exclusion fear
5. "How long does HRD Corp approval take?" → de-risk the process

## VISUAL DESIGN STANDARDS

|---------|--------|-----|

| Primary CTA buttons | Gold #F0A500 | Malaysian cultural resonance, premium, action |
| Secondary accents, success | Teal #0FA89A | Forward-looking, innovation |
| Problem/danger | Red #DC2626 | Loss aversion trigger |
| Savings/gain | Green #16A34A | Money, growth |
| Section backgrounds | Alternate white / cool grey / navy dark | Creates rhythm, prevents fatigue |

### Typography for Authority

```
Headline rule: Plus Jakarta Sans or Sora, 700–800 weight, tight line-height (1.15–1.25)
Body rule: Inter, 400 weight, 1.6–1.7 line height, 16px minimum
Number rule: Tabular numbers, extrabold (800), no letter-spacing
Badge/label rule: 0.7–0.8rem, 700 weight, uppercase, wide tracking
```

### Layout Rhythm

```
Hero section: 100px top, 80px bottom
Standard sections: 80px top and bottom
Small sections: 48px top and bottom
Card padding: 28–32px
Grid gap: 24–32px
Max content column: 620px for readability
Max page width: 1120px
```

### Trust Signal Placement

```
Fold 1 (above scroll): Brand name + government/certification logos
Fold 2 (problem section): Verified data with source attribution
Fold 3 (solution): Social proof (stat OR testimonial teaser)
Fold 4 (programs): Pricing + HRD Corp claimable badge
Fold 5 (CTA): Risk reversal + response time guarantee
```

## QUALITY CHECKLIST BEFORE SHIPPING

**5-second test:** Cover the headline. Can a stranger still tell what this is? ✓/✗
**Specificity test:** Count the vague words ("solutions", "innovative", "seamless"). Replace each with a number or fact.
**Mobile CTA test:** On a phone, is the WhatsApp button immediately visible without scrolling?
**Trust density:** Count trust signals per scroll screen. Target: 2–3 per screen.
**Load speed:** Fonts only from Google Fonts. Zero external JS libraries. Images optimised.
**Contrast ratio:** All body text ≥ 4.5:1. All heading text ≥ 3:1. Test with browser DevTools.
