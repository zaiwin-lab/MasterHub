Pengenalan Syarikat

A modern dessert brand, made with heart

Snow Buko & Desserts is a modern dessert brand focused on buko, biscuits, cakes and more. Our flagship product is a creamy buko dessert combined with fresh fruit.

Founded by Puan Sharifah Norliyana Binti Wan Seh, 30, with a background in Diploma Business Management — Snow Buko was built to bring an affordable, premium dessert experience to everyone.

Our concept is simple: easy to find, sweet, fresh and premium — suitable for every age group, men, women, and children alike.

Easy to Find. Available across bazaars, events and partner outlets throughout Sarawak.

Sweet, Fresh & Premium. A balanced, creamy taste made with quality fresh fruit — never overly sweet.

For Everyone. A treat suitable for all ages — men, women and children alike.
