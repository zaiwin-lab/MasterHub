# Product

## Register
brand (one-page portal), with a product-register admin table and payment status screens.

## Users and purpose
UTHM alumni in Sarawak and Borneo, UTHM stakeholders, corporate contributors and industry partners. Most arrive on a phone from a WhatsApp or social link. The job: understand who the chapter is, trust it, and pay a contribution or programme fee in under two minutes, then get a receipt by email.

## Brand personality
Steady, civic, warm. Institutional enough for a corporate finance officer, personal enough for a fellow alumnus.

## Anti-references
Busy university portals, charity sites with countdowns and urgency, generic AI landing pages (gradient text, icon-card grids, eyebrow labels on every section). Nothing that implies this portal is an official UTHM system.

## Design principles
1. The contribution form is the product. Everything above it builds the trust needed to complete it.
2. Transparency without alarm: the KOBIS Berhad arrangement is stated plainly and calmly, right before payment.
3. Navy carries the identity; white space carries the credibility. One photo of Sarawak, no stock people.
4. No fake confirmations. Every "paid" state is verified with ToyyibPay.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard flow through the form, visible focus, labelled fields with inline errors, reduced-motion support, 16px+ inputs on mobile.
