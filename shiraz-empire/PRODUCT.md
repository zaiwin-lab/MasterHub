# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homeowners, business owners, and event organizers in Kota Samarahan and Greater Kuching, Sarawak who need air-conditioning supply, installation, maintenance, or repair. They're searching locally (Google/Facebook/WhatsApp) for a trustworthy, responsive technician — often during a hot, urgent moment (AC broken, new unit needed). They want clear pricing, fast response, and confidence the job will be done properly.

## Product Purpose

Shiraz Empire is a registered local aircon service provider (supply, install, maintenance, repair) based in Kota Samarahan. Success looks like visitors immediately trusting the brand as professional and locally rooted, understanding the four core services, and clicking through to book a service or get a quote via WhatsApp/contact.

## Brand Personality

Cool, crisp, trustworthy. Clean and modern with a "frost/cool comfort" identity — professional tradesman energy, not flashy. Confident and capable without feeling corporate or impersonal; proudly Sarawakian and local.

## Anti-references

- Generic flat corporate SaaS templates (cookie-cutter business sites, stock blue gradients, lifeless icon grids).
- Overly playful or childish — this is a service business, trust matters.
- Cold/sterile minimalism that hides the local, personal, family-business warmth.

## Design Principles

- Trust-first visual language: registration details, founder, and service area should feel credible and prominent.
- Cool comfort identity: lean into the "frost/cooling" motif (ice blues, crisp whites, subtle frost/snowflake details) without becoming a winter cliché.
- Real-world proof: real photos of technicians at work, aircon units, and installations build more trust than icons/illustrations alone.
- Local pride: Sarawakian/Kota Samarahan identity should be visible and warm, not generic.
- Mobile-first polish: most visitors search and call from mobile.
- Keep the existing structure (sections, i18n, ticker bar) but elevate execution to premium-craft level.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Maintain WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body text).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all frost/orb/reveal animations.
- Preserve existing 4-language i18n support (BM, EN, ZH, IB).
