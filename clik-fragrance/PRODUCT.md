# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Young, social-media-native consumers (mostly Gen-Z, Malaysia/Sarawak-based and beyond) who discover the brand through TikTok/Instagram. They're browsing on mobile, often mid-scroll, drawn in by mood/identity-based positioning rather than traditional fragrance notes. They want something affordable, fun, and easy to try (10ml travel-size).

## Product Purpose

Clik Fragrance sells mood-based, travel-size (10ml) perfumes — pick a scent based on how you want to feel (confidence, calm, attraction, focus, joy), not by traditional fragrance categories. Success looks like visitors immediately feeling the brand's energy, understanding the mood-matching concept, and clicking through to WhatsApp/Instagram to order.

## Brand Personality

Playful, bold, Gen-Z. Energetic and vibrant — leans into youth culture, TikTok content-creator energy, GRWM/"scent of the day" vibes. Confident without being exclusive or luxury-gatekeeping; approachable and fun, not childish.

## Anti-references

- Generic luxury-perfume cliches (gold foil, black/gold elegance, French maison vibes) — Clik is affordable and everyday, not aspirational-luxury.
- Flat corporate SaaS aesthetics (cream/sand backgrounds, generic card grids, tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section).
- Anything that feels sterile, clinical, or overly minimal — the brand is about energy and self-expression.

## Design Principles

- Mood-first visual language: each of the 5 collections (BOLD, SERENE, MUSE, CLARITY, SUNNY) should feel visually distinct and emotionally legible at a glance.
- Content-creator energy: the site should feel like it could live inside a TikTok GRWM video — dynamic, current, shareable.
- Affordable confidence: bold and playful, never gatekeeping or precious.
- Mobile-first polish: most visitors arrive from social on mobile; every interaction must shine there first.
- Keep the existing structure (sections, i18n, sparkle/bottle motif) but elevate execution to premium-craft level.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Maintain WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body text) despite a vibrant palette.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all sparkle/orb/reveal animations.
- Preserve existing 4-language i18n support (BM, EN, ZH, IB).
